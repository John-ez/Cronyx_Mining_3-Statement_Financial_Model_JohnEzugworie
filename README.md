# Cronyx Mining LTD 3-Statement Financial Model
This project presents an Excel-based three-statement financial model developed for Cronyx Mining Ltd., a hypothetical mining company operating in Nigeria. The model forecasts the company's financial performance and position over a four-year period, from 2026 to 2029, using operational assumptions and financial projections.

The model integrates the Income Statement, Statement of Financial Position, and Cash Flow Statement to demonstrate how operational activities, profitability, working capital, capital expenditure, and financing decisions affect the company's overall financial performance and cash position.

**Project purpose**: Learning and demonstration of financial modelling skills.

---

## Objectives
- Develop an integrated three-statement financial model.
- Translate operational assumptions into revenue and cost projections.
- Forecast profitability, assets, liabilities, equity, and cash flows.
- Demonstrate financial modelling best practices, including formula-driven calculations, linked financial statements, and balance sheet checks.

---

## Key Modelling Components

## 1. Revenue Forecasting
Revenue projections are driven by operational assumptions relating to the company's mining and logistics activities, including:
- Kaolin sales volumes and selling prices.
- Logistics volumes and prices per ton.
- Annual revenue growth and price escalation assumptions.

## 2. Cost and Expenses Forecasting
The model incorporates assumptions for major operating costs and expenses, including:
- Direct mining and production costs.
- Diesel and other operational costs.
- Office and administrative expenses.
- Payroll and insurance expenses.
- Depreciation and amortization.
- Interest expenses and income tax.

## 3. Integrated Three Statement Modelling
The three financial statements are linked through formulas to demonstrate the flow of financial information across the model.
- **Income Statement**: Calculates profitability and retained earnings.
![Cronyx_Income_Statement_Preview](Cronyx_Mining_IncomeStatement_Preview_JohnEzugworie.png)

- **Statement of Financial Position**: Reflects projected assets, liabilities, and shareholders' equity.
![Cronyx_Balance_Sheet_Preview](Cronyx_Mining_BalanceSheet(SOFP)_Preview_JohnEzugworie.png)

- **Cash Flow Statement**: Reconciles cash movements from operating, investing, and financing activities to derive closing cash balances.
![Cronyx_Cashflow_Statement_Preview](Cronyx_Mining_CashflowStatement_Preview_JohnEzugworie.png)

## 4. Financing and Capital Expenditure
The model incorporates assumptions for capital expenditure, equity injections, term loans, principal repayments, and interest payments. These inputs help illustrate how investment and financing decisions influence the company's cash position and financial structure.

## 5. Financial Model Checks
The Statement of Financial Position includes a balance check comparing total assets with total liabilities and equity. This helps identify inconsistencies in the model and supports the validation of the projected financial position.

## Conclusion and Disclaimer
This model was developed for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes only. The projections depend on the assumptions embedded in the workbook and should not be interpreted as actual company results, investment advice, or a validated forecast of future performance.

The model's outputs are sensitive to changes in operating volumes, selling prices, production costs, financing assumptions, and other inputs. Independent validation and appropriate supporting information would be required before using the model for business planning, fundraising, valuation, or investment decisions.
